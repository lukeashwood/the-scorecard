What you're looking at

The Scorecard's measures are grouped into eight categories — cost of living, energy, housing and so on. Here, each category is a sphere, sized to match the centre sphere: a sphere's colour and glow show how many of its measures are rated off track, on a scale from green (mostly on track) through amber to red (mostly off track). Each small dot orbiting a sphere is one individual measure, coloured the same way. The centre sphere is every measure combined.

Nothing here is opinion — every number is pulled directly from the Scorecard, which sources and checks each figure daily against the ABS, the Reserve Bank and Treasury. Click "Open on the Scorecard" in any panel to see the full detail, sources and method for a measure.
