How this page works

The list is compiled by hand from the Parliament of Australia's bill pages and legislation.gov.au, and each entry shows the date it was last verified. The daily automated check tests every link on this page and flags any entry whose status is due for re-checking. Summaries describe what a law does in plain English. A "controversy" note is included only where named organisations, parliamentarians or committees publicly objected; it reports their position, and the government's stated reason, with links to the original statements. It is not the site's own judgement. "What the public said" reports measurable evidence of public opinion: published polls, parliamentary or public petitions and their signature counts, protest turnouts, inquiry submission numbers and referendum results, each linked to its source. Every entry has a "Have your say" button that sends your view to the site's team. Think something is missing or wrong? Report it or suggest an addition.

Bill status, dates and titles: Parliament of Australia (aph.gov.au) and the Federal Register of Legislation, as linked on each entry. Controversy notes cite the organisations and parliamentarians quoted.
