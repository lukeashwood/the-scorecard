Don't take our word for it

Every number, every source.

We don't make up numbers. Every figure on this site comes from an official publication you can open yourself, and our systems re-check the data every day.

How the data is verified

Most figures come straight from the machine-readable data services of the Australian Bureau of Statistics and the Reserve Bank of Australia. Nobody types them in. Every day an automated process:

  1. Downloads the latest official data files directly from the ABS and RBA.
  2. Recalculates every figure, benchmark and rating from that raw data.
  3. Cross-checks key numbers against a second independent official source. For example, RBA and ABS inflation must agree, and the unemployment rate must match in both. If they don't agree, the figure isn't published.
  4. Range-checks every value, so a corrupted download can't produce an impossible number.
  5. Checks freshness, flagging any source that has stopped updating.
  6. Tests every source link cited on the site.

If a check fails, the site keeps showing the last verified figure with a clear "under review" notice. It never shows a number it couldn't confirm.

Some figures, such as Budget papers, energy regulator decisions and public service headcounts, aren't published as data feeds. These are marked Hand-verified and show the date they were checked against the original document. Each one has a re-check date tied to the source's next release, and the daily process flags any that are overdue.

How ratings work

Each measure is rated On track, Watch or Off track against a benchmark stated on the card: the government's own target or promise, the Reserve Bank's target, the long-run average, or where things stood when the government took office in May 2022. The exact rule for each rating is published under every measure. Measures where more or less isn't clearly better are marked Context and not rated. We also show results that reflect well on the government.

Corrections

If you believe a figure is wrong, email with the measure and the source you're relying on. We will check it against the official source and correct any error promptly.
