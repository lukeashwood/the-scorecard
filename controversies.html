How this page works

An entry is included only when a minister's own conduct was publicly questioned and the episode is documented in reputable reporting or official records: Hansard, Senate estimates, the Independent Parliamentary Expenses Authority, the National Anti-Corruption Commission, or established news organisations. Each entry gives the facts as reported, the minister's or government's response, and the outcome: an apology, repayment, resignation, referral, official finding, or none. Categories describe the kind of question raised, not a verdict. Rumours, satire and unverified social-media claims are not included. Entries show the date they were last verified, and the daily automated check tests every link. Know of something that should be here, or think an entry is wrong? Suggest it or report it.

Facts, responses and outcomes are drawn from the sources linked on each entry. Categories are descriptive labels, not findings by this site.
